Florida man hides underwater with orange hose, leads deputies straight to him

He kept moving the hose to make his hiding place less obvious.

A Florida man trying to avoid deputies submerged himself in a shallow pond and breathed through a bright orange garden hose that stuck out of the water above him. He stayed close to the bank, where he could lie flat on the bottom while keeping the short hose in his mouth.

Deputies searching the bank spotted the orange tip standing several inches above the surface. When they moved toward it, the man pushed it into a patch of reeds. The hose drew a line through the water as it moved, and the reeds shook after everything around them had gone still.

“If my head’s under, you can’t see me,” the man said when deputies called for him to come out. He tried once more to move the hose behind the reeds without surfacing. Deputies followed the fresh ripples to where he was lying and told him to stand. He came up holding the hose, its orange end still above the water.
