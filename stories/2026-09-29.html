Florida man drills holes to drain his kayak, then launches it and sinks it

He widened two of the holes after the first test.

A Florida man drilled six holes through the floor of his kayak to drain rainwater, then launched it at a boat ramp to check his work. The sit-inside kayak had collected water while stored outdoors. He set it upright on his driveway, drilled through the lowest part of the cockpit and watched the puddle run out.

At the ramp, water came back through the holes as soon as he pushed off. He paddled about 20 feet before the cockpit filled over his shoes and the kayak settled onto the bottom in waist-deep water. He dragged it to shore. “The holes work,” he said. “They just need to drain faster.”

He got a cordless drill from his truck and widened two of the holes. On his second launch, water rose through the floor before he could sit down. He pulled the kayak onto the concrete, where it drained through the holes again.
