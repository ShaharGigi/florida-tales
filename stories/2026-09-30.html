Florida man pours concrete into running toilet, sends water up shower drain

He left the water on to push the concrete toward the leak.

A Florida man poured quick-set concrete into his running toilet Tuesday, then flushed it twice to push the mixture toward what he thought was a leak in the drain.

The toilet had been running since morning. Instead of closing the supply valve, he mixed a bag of concrete in a bucket and emptied it into the bowl. The first flush carried some of it past the toilet. The second left a gray ring of mixture in the bowl, which began to harden.

Water soon started bubbling through the shower drain, where the toilet’s waste pipe joined the same line. The man kept the supply valve open after noticing the backup. “The water has to push it to the leak,” he said.

By the time he shut off the valve, the toilet could no longer flush, and water stood two inches deep in the shower. A plumber found hardened concrete in the toilet trap and the shared drain. Both fixtures had to be removed to reach the blockage.
