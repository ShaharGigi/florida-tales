Florida man holds up mirror to hide from store camera, shows face while stealing it

He believed the mirror would send the camera’s view back into its lens.

A Florida man tried to hide from a convenience-store camera by holding up a hand mirror, then stole it while the camera recorded his face in the glass. He had taken the mirror from a display beside the register and stood beneath the camera before lifting it toward the lens.

He kept the reflective side facing the camera, but tilted the mirror back so he could check its position. From the camera’s angle, that tilt put his face in the reflection. He looked into the mirror twice, each time holding it still long enough for a clear image. “It can’t see me if it sees itself,” he said as he adjusted it.

He carried the mirror out without paying. The store’s recording showed him entering with his face partly obscured by a cap; the clearest view came from the mirror he took.
