Florida man unbolts parking meter to fight ticket, gets arrested after bringing it to police

He brought a socket wrench and a hand truck to collect his evidence.

A Florida man unbolted a parking meter from the sidewalk and wheeled it into a police station to contest a $25 ticket. He said the meter had refused his quarter that morning and wanted officers to inspect it before anyone could repair it.

He had removed four bolts from the base and strapped the meter to a hand truck. At the station, he parked it beside the front desk and handed over his citation. The meter number printed on the ticket matched the number on its pole.

“If I leave it out there, they can say it worked the whole time,” he told the desk officer. He then put a quarter in the meter to demonstrate the problem. Its display added 15 minutes.

The officer kept the meter as evidence and called city maintenance about the exposed mounting plate. The man left with the parking ticket still in force and a charge for taking city property.
