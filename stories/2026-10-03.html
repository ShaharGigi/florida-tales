Florida man tests foam trap for porch thieves and seals his own front door shut

He placed the package against the door to see whether the foam would mark a thief’s shoes.

A Florida man testing a package meant to stop porch thieves set it off on his own doorstep and sealed his front door shut.

He had put a can of expanding foam inside a cardboard box and rigged the lid to press its nozzle when opened. A short tube pointed toward the bottom of the door. He placed the box against the threshold because he wanted the foam to stick to a thief’s shoes before the person could leave.

Standing outside with the door closed, he lifted the lid to check the trigger. Foam ran across the threshold and into the narrow gap beneath the door. He pulled the box away but left the foam in place while he went around through the garage for a scraper.

By the time he returned, the foam had hardened along the bottom edge. The door would not open. “That’s where they’d be standing,” he said, pointing to the sealed threshold. He used the garage entrance while he cut the foam away.
