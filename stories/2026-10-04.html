Florida man chains steering wheel to parking meter to stop thieves, then drives off dragging meter

He kept the chain on because he wanted the car protected while he drove.

A Florida man chained his steering wheel to a parking meter to stop car thieves, then drove away with the meter dragging behind him. He had fed the chain through his open driver’s window and around the wheel while he ran into a store. When he returned, he started the car without removing it.

The chain went taut at the end of the parking space. The man pressed the accelerator again, pulling the meter’s mounting bolts out of the sidewalk. He drove half a block with the post scraping behind his door before stopping to inspect the noise. The chain had also kept him from turning the wheel far enough to pull over neatly.

He said he had noticed the chain but meant to leave it in place. “Cars get stolen at stoplights, too,” he said. He unhooked the meter only after finding a place to park, then wound the chain back around the steering wheel.
