Florida man chains tow truck to his bumper to stop tow, gets both vehicles hauled away

He refused to unlock the chain when a larger trailer arrived.

A Florida man chained a tow truck to his car’s bumper-mounted hitch Tuesday to keep the truck from removing his car from a shopping center fire lane.

The driver had stepped out to check the car’s plate when the man looped a chain through the truck’s towing frame and secured it with a padlock. He kept the key in his pocket. “They can’t take mine if theirs has to come too,” he told the driver. When asked to unlock it, he pulled the chain tight to show that both vehicles were still attached.

The tow company sent a recovery vehicle with a long flatbed trailer. Its crew loaded the car and the tow truck onto the same trailer, leaving the chain in place. At the storage lot, the man was told his car could not be released until he provided the key and paid for its tow. He handed over the key but asked for the chain back.
