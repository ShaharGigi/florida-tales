Florida man pulls off front door with excavator, collapses porch roof and leaves house wide open

The homeowner had left his keys inside and had an excavator parked in the yard.

A Florida man locked out of his house used an excavator to pull off the front door, bringing down the porch roof above it. The machine was parked in his yard for a drainage project, with its key still in the ignition.

He hooked the bucket under the door handle and pulled. The door came free with part of its frame, but the bucket also caught a porch post. When the post gave way, the roof dropped across the front steps, blocking the opening he had just made.

Rather than leave the machine and call for help, he used the bucket to drag the fallen roofing off the steps. “I was already most of the way in,” he said. The second pull tore away the remaining door frame and a section of siding. He walked inside to retrieve his keys, then found there was nothing left at the entrance that he could close.
