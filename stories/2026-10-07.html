Florida man steals ATM for cash, calls 911 for a tow and leads deputies to it

He waited on top of the machine so the tow driver could find it.

A Florida man pulled an ATM from a convenience store to get its cash, then called 911 when he could not haul the machine away. He had wrapped a tow strap around the freestanding ATM and dragged it through the front doors with his pickup, leaving a cracked glass panel behind.

The strap snapped when he tried to pull the machine into the truck bed. Rather than leave, he called 911 and requested a tow truck capable of lifting “bank equipment.” He gave the store address and said he would wait on the machine so the driver could find it.

When asked whether he owned the ATM, he said, “I will once I get it home.” He sat on its casing and tried to pry open the cash compartment with a tire iron.

Deputies arrived instead of a tow truck. They found the broken strap tied to his pickup and the tire iron wedged under the ATM’s locked panel. He asked whether they could move it before taking him away.
