Florida man handcuffs himself to steering wheel to stop repossession, gets towed away with car

He left the handcuff key inside his apartment.

A Florida man handcuffed himself to his steering wheel Monday to stop a repo crew from taking his car, then remained inside as the crew towed it away. He had parked nose-first against a curb and fastened one cuff around his wrist and the other through a steering-wheel spoke. He also buckled his seat belt and left the cuff key in his apartment.

When the tow operator arrived, the man refused to unlock himself. “If I stay with it, they can’t take it,” he said. The operator raised the car’s front wheels and began pulling it across the apartment parking lot, with the man still in the driver’s seat.

At the lot exit, the operator stopped and told him to get out. The man could not reach the seat-belt release with his cuffed hand and would not let the operator call someone for the key. He was still attached to the wheel when the tow truck pulled onto the road.
