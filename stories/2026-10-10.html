Florida man ties screen door to his belt to stop it slamming, gets pulled into trash bin

After the first gust dragged him across the porch, he shortened the cord.

A Florida man trying to stop his screen door from slamming hooked its handle to his belt with a bungee cord and ended up in his trash bin when the wind caught the door.

He had been carrying groceries through the doorway and wanted both hands free. He clipped one end of the cord to the outside handle and the other to a loop on his belt, then stood on the porch to test it. The first gust pulled him several feet toward the door before the cord stretched enough for it to slam anyway.

He shortened the cord and tried again. “It just needed less slack,” he said. He left the trash bin beside the porch with its lid open, where he had been clearing out a bag of packaging.

The next gust swung the door wide. The shortened cord pulled him off balance; he caught his foot on a bin wheel and fell through the open lid. The screen door swung back and slammed while he was still inside.
