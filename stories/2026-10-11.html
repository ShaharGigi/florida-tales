Florida man rams stuck garage door with SUV and wedges SUV beneath it

He hit the door a second time after the first impact knocked it off its tracks.

A Florida man drove his SUV into his jammed garage door, then hit it again after the first impact loosened the door. The second hit folded a section onto the SUV’s roof and left the vehicle stuck halfway out of the garage.

The door had lifted about three inches before stopping. Instead of pulling the opener’s release cord, he backed the SUV toward the far wall and drove its front bumper into the bottom panel. The panel bowed toward the driveway, and two rollers came out of the side track.

He reversed a few feet, leaving the bent panel hanging in the opening. “It moved the first time,” he said before driving forward again. The bottom panel folded farther outward, while the loose section above it tipped inward and settled across the windshield and roof rack.

He got out through the driver’s door and pulled the release cord. With the opener detached, the section resting on the roof rack dropped another inch.
