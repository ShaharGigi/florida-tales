Florida man chains car to front door to stop theft, tears door off when he drives away

He kept the door attached after stopping to inspect the damage.

A Florida man tore his front door from its frame when he drove away with his car chained to it to prevent theft. He had run the chain from a tow hook beneath the rear bumper through two holes he drilled in the wooden door, then secured it with a padlock.

The chain went tight at the end of the driveway. He pressed the accelerator, splitting the doorframe and pulling the door off its hinges. It landed flat and scraped along the pavement behind the car. He stopped after a neighbor pointed at it, checked that the padlock was still closed and picked a loose hinge screw out of the chain.

He decided not to disconnect it. “If I take it off, the car isn’t protected,” he said. He drove to a hardware store for longer screws with the door dragging behind him. The chain was still locked when he parked.
