Florida man drills second hole in leaking boat to balance water, sinks it at dock

He widened the new hole after the boat briefly sat level.

A Florida man drilled a second hole below the waterline of his leaking boat at a dock, saying he wanted the water inside to spread evenly. He had found a slow leak near the left side of the hull after stepping aboard and seeing water collect under a bench. He used a cordless drill to put a matching hole through the right side.

The new opening sent a steady stream into the boat. As water spread across the floor, the hull briefly sat level. “That means it’s working,” he said. When the boat started leaning again, he widened the new hole. Water covered the floorboards and reached the drill battery, which he lifted onto the dock.

He stepped off as the boat settled lower against its mooring line. The hull came to rest on the shallow bottom with both openings underwater. He left the drill on the dock and told a marina worker the second hole had been too small.
