Florida man chains boat trailer to garage door, tears door off while driving away

He left the chain attached because he wanted the trailer secured for the whole trip.

A Florida man chained his boat trailer to his garage door to prevent theft, then pulled the door off its tracks while leaving for the boat ramp. He had looped the chain around the trailer frame and the door’s bottom rail the night before, leaving enough slack to raise the door with its remote.

With the boat hitched to his truck, he drove until the chain went tight. The bottom panel bent, and the trailer stopped short of the street. He got out, saw that the chain was still fastened at both ends and returned to the truck. “If I take it off now, the trailer isn’t secured,” he said.

On his second attempt, the door’s rollers came out of the tracks. He dragged the door down the driveway behind the trailer before stopping at the curb. He unhitched the trailer to clear the driveway, leaving it beside the detached door with the chain still in place.
