Florida man chains boat to dock to prevent theft, then drags dock into canal

He left the chain on for a trip to the fuel pump.

A Florida man dragged a floating dock into a canal after starting his boat without removing the chain he had installed to keep it from being stolen. He had looped the chain through the dock’s metal frame and locked the other end to a cleat on his boat.

The man was headed to a fuel pump across the canal. When the chain pulled tight, he backed up, then tried forward gear, believing the boat was caught on one of the dock’s mooring ropes. The motor pulled both ropes loose from their shore cleats. A short ramp slid off the dock and dropped into the water.

The man cut the engine when he saw the dock following him. He reached the shore by pulling on the chain, but left the padlock in place. “If I unlock it every time I go out, it’s not doing anything,” he said.

He waited on the boat while the dock drifted against a canal wall, still chained to his stern cleat.
