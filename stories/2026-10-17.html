Florida man chains pickup to loose parking block, breaks windshield when tow truck lifts it

The concrete block came off the pavement before the pickup left the lot.

A Florida man chained his pickup to a concrete parking block to stop a repossession, then watched the block swing into his windshield when a tow truck lifted the pickup. He had run the chain from the block, which sat in front of the hood, to a ladder rack above the cab.

The man told the tow operator the block would keep the pickup in place. But its mounting bolts were gone. When the operator raised the rear of the truck, the chain tightened, dragged the block across the hood and pulled it upward. It struck the windshield hard enough to crack the glass across the driver’s side.

The operator lowered the pickup so the chain could be removed. The man pulled the block back toward its original spot and asked for another chance to secure it. “It’ll hold if you don’t lift it,” he said. The operator removed the chain, loaded the pickup and left the block beside two empty bolt holes.
