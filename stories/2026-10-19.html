Florida man drills holes in overflowing toilet tank, floods bathroom faster

He kept the water supply on so the toilet would still flush.

A Florida man drilled a hole in the side of his overflowing toilet tank, sending water across his bathroom floor. The tank’s fill valve had stuck open, and he wanted to lower the water before it reached the rim.

He put a bucket beneath the hole, but it filled while the tank kept refilling. Rather than turn off the supply valve behind the toilet, he drilled a second hole on the other side of the tank. Water from that opening ran behind the toilet and under the bathroom door.

“If I turn it off, I can’t flush,” he said. He moved the bucket between the two holes until water began reaching the hallway.

A repair worker shut off the supply valve. With the tank emptied, both holes were visible below the level the water needed to reach for a flush.
