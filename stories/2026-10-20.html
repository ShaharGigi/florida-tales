Florida man sets off smoke bomb to prove detector is broken, evacuates apartment building

He kept the windows shut so his landlord could inspect the evidence.

A Florida man set off a smoke bomb beneath his apartment’s ceiling detector to prove to his landlord that it did not work, triggering an evacuation of the building. He had complained that the detector chirped at night and believed the chirp meant it could no longer sense smoke. He put the bomb in a metal baking pan, shut the windows and aimed his phone at the ceiling.

The detector sounded within seconds. Connected alarms began sounding in the other apartments, and residents went to the parking lot. When the landlord arrived, the man showed him the recording. “It wasn’t doing that before I made the smoke,” he said.

He refused to open the windows, saying the landlord needed to see how much smoke the detector had missed. Smoke drifted into the hallway through the gap beneath his door, keeping residents outside while the landlord opened the windows and aired out the floor. The man continued recording the detector as it sounded.
