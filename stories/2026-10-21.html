Florida man chains car to tow truck to stop repossession, sends car to lot with chain attached

He refused to remove the padlock before the driver loaded the car.

A Florida man chained his car to a tow truck to stop a repossession, then refused to unlock it as the driver prepared to take the car away.

The man threaded a chain through the car’s front tow eye and around the truck’s hitch, securing it with a padlock. “He can’t tow a car that’s locked to his truck,” he told the driver. When asked to remove the chain, he pulled it tight to show that the lock held.

The driver raised the car’s front wheels onto the wheel lift and fastened the usual straps. He secured the loose length of chain so it would not drag, then drove to the impound lot with the car still attached to the hitch. The man followed in another car, carrying the padlock key.

At the lot, he offered to unlock the chain if the driver released his car. The car stayed, and the man left with the only key.
