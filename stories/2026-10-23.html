Florida man chains ladder to pickup to prevent theft, then drives off and tears gutter from house

He left the ladder propped against the roof when he attached it to the truck.

A Florida man chained his ladder to his pickup to keep it from being stolen, then drove away while it was still leaning against his house. He had passed the chain through the ladder’s bottom rung and locked it to the trailer hitch before leaving for the hardware store.

The chain pulled the ladder’s feet down the driveway. Its top caught behind the aluminum gutter and tore several brackets loose before falling. The man stopped when he heard the ladder scraping the pavement behind the truck. The chain and lock were still secure.

He pulled the ladder to the side of the driveway but did not unlock it. “If I take it off the truck, someone can take it,” he said. He then drove forward to clear the fallen gutter. The ladder slid after him, caught the mailbox post and pulled its mounting bolts from the ground. He parked with the ladder still chained to the hitch.
