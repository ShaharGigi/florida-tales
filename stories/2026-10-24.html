Florida man tests repaired brakes on boat ramp, drives pickup into lake twice

He backed out after the first failed test and tried again.

A Florida man drove his pickup down a boat ramp to test its newly repaired brakes, then backed out of the lake and repeated the test. He had replaced a leaking brake line that morning and wanted to check the repair before driving home.

He started near the top of the ramp and pressed the pedal halfway down. It sank to the floor. The pickup rolled until its front tires were underwater, but he put it in reverse and backed up the ramp before the engine stalled.

The man checked the reservoir, found it full and decided he might not have pressed the pedal firmly enough. “I need to give them a fair test,” he said. He drove down the ramp again, this time starting farther uphill. The pedal went to the floor again, and the pickup entered deep enough water to stall.

A tow truck pulled it out with water draining from the cab. The man asked the driver to leave it at the top of the ramp so he could check the brakes once the engine dried.
