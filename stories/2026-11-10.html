Florida man uses leaf blower to unclog toilet, sprays ceiling and leaves clog in place

He tried to seal the blower nozzle with a bath towel before making a second attempt.

A Florida man trying to clear a clogged toilet aimed a running leaf blower into the bowl, sending water and wet toilet paper onto the bathroom ceiling without moving the blockage. He had set aside a plunger after deciding the blower would push the clog through the drain.

He held the nozzle below the rim and packed a bath towel around it to keep air from escaping. Water surged over the rim, carrying the towel partway into the bowl. Strips of wet paper stuck to the ceiling light and the exhaust fan cover.

He pulled out the towel, wiped the nozzle and tried again from the other side of the bowl. “The air needs a better seal,” he said. The second burst soaked the towel and added another layer of paper to the ceiling. With the toilet still clogged, he began wrapping the wet towel more tightly around the nozzle.
