Florida man sprays foam down sink drain to stop leak, blocks drain and floods bathroom

He left the faucet running to test the repair.

A Florida man trying to stop a bathroom sink leak sprayed expanding foam down the drain, blocked it and flooded the room while testing his repair.

The man had noticed water dripping from a pipe joint beneath the sink whenever he ran the faucet. He removed the drain stopper, emptied a can of foam into the opening and left the tap running to see whether the drip had stopped. It had, because water could no longer reach the pipe.

As the basin filled, he saw water enter the small overflow opening near its rim. He sprayed foam into that opening too. “It was still finding a way out,” he said. Water then spilled over the counter, soaked the cabinet and spread across the bathroom floor.

After shutting off the faucet, he put a bucket under the dry pipe joint. The foam remained in both openings, and the sink would not drain.
