Florida man welds front door shut to stop eviction, calls 911 to have it cut open

Firefighters opened the apartment’s only door while deputies waited outside.

A Florida man welded his apartment door to its steel frame to stop deputies from carrying out an eviction, then called 911 because he could not get out. He had run a line of weld along the latch side and across the top, leaving the hinges attached. The apartment’s windows had fixed security bars.

When deputies arrived, he spoke to them through the closed door and said the weld was keeping them outside. Minutes later, he reported that he was trapped. Firefighters arrived with a cutting tool, but he asked them to leave the weld intact. “If you cut that part, they can open it,” he told them.

The firefighters cut through the welded seam and pulled the door open. The man stepped into the hallway carrying the welding mask he had used. The deputies, still waiting beside the door, entered to carry out the eviction.
