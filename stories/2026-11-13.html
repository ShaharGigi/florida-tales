Florida man hides from deputies in trash bin, gets dumped into garbage truck and gives away his location on 911

The truck had traveled one block when he called for help.

A Florida man hiding from deputies climbed into a curbside trash bin on collection morning and was dumped into the hopper of a garbage truck. The truck’s mechanical arm lifted the bin while deputies searched the other side of the street.

The man landed on a pile of flattened boxes with his phone still in his hand. After the truck traveled one block, he called 911 and said he had gotten away from the deputies but needed help getting out before the compactor ran. He gave the dispatcher the name of the street he could see through the open hopper.

The driver heard him banging on the truck, stopped and shut down the equipment. The man told the dispatcher he had chosen the bin because “they wouldn’t check it once the truck picked it up.”

The deputies arrived at the stopped truck and helped him climb down. The bin was back at the curb, empty.
