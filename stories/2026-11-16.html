Florida man steals tow truck to avoid towing fee, shreds his sedan’s tires while hauling it away

The sedan remained attached to the truck, with its parking brake on.

A Florida man drove off in the tow truck lifting his sedan, dragging the car through two blocks of traffic to avoid a towing fee. The truck’s driver had stepped out to photograph the sedan after securing its front wheels to the lift. The man climbed into the idling truck before the driver returned.

He had left the sedan’s parking brake on. Its rear tires scraped along the pavement as he drove, leaving two black lines from the parking lot to a gas station. There, he stopped beside an air pump and tried to lower the wheel lift using controls in the truck’s cab.

The tow truck driver caught up on foot and told him to leave the equipment alone. “If I bring it back myself, it wasn’t a tow,” the man said. He then drove back toward the parking lot with the sedan still attached. By the time he stopped, both rear tires had torn open, and the sedan was resting on its rims.
