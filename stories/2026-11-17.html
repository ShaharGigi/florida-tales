Florida man chains trash bin to porch to stop it blowing away; garbage truck pulls railing into street

The chain reached from the curb to two porch posts.

A Florida man chained his trash bin to his porch to keep it from blowing away, and a garbage truck pulled part of the railing into the street during pickup. He had left enough chain for the bin to reach the curb but had wrapped the other end around two porch posts.

The truck’s automated arm gripped the bin and raised it before the chain pulled tight. Both posts broke loose at their bases. The driver stopped with the bin overhead and a section of railing hanging below it.

The homeowner said wind had tipped the empty bin twice that week. He had threaded the chain around the bin’s metal wheel axle because a previous attempt to tie its plastic handle to the porch had slipped. “The handle was the weak part,” he said.

After the crew lowered the bin and freed the railing, he rolled it back to the curb. By afternoon, he had chained the axle to the porch’s remaining post.
