Florida man chains pickup to garage post to stop repossession, brings roof down on pickup

The chain held after the post came loose.

A Florida man chained his pickup’s rear axle to a steel garage support post to stop a tow truck from taking it, then watched the post pull part of the roof down onto the pickup.

The tow driver had backed up to the open garage and begun pulling the pickup toward the driveway. The chain went taut, but its padlock held. Instead, bolts at the base of the post tore free, and the roof above the garage opening dropped onto the pickup’s cab. The tow driver stopped before the vehicle reached the driveway.

The man said he had chosen the post because it was attached to the house. “They can’t take the house,” he said. He declined to unlock the chain while the driver waited for the fallen roofing to be cleared.

After the tow truck left, the man put a second padlock through the chain. The pickup remained in the garage beneath the collapsed roof, still attached to the displaced post.
