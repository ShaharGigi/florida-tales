Florida man reports stolen cocaine, asks responding officer whether this conversation is confidential

He wanted a case number but asked that the case not contain his name.

A Florida man called police to report that someone had stolen cocaine from his apartment, then asked the responding officer whether their conversation was confidential.

The man said he had invited four people over Saturday and noticed the bag missing after the last guest left. He had already searched the couch, called each guest twice and sent a group message giving the thief until noon to return it without consequences.

At 12:03, he called police.

The officer asked what had been taken. The man requested that the report say “personal property.” Asked to describe the property, he lowered his voice and checked whether the officer’s body camera was on.

“I’m the victim in this,” he said.

He then offered to show the officer another bag so there would be no confusion about what to look for. When the officer asked where it was, the man said he would need an assurance that it would be returned.

He also asked whether recovering the missing bag meant he would get both back.
