Florida man brings stolen speed-limit sign to court to prove road had no speed limit

He arrived with a photograph of the empty pole and the sign wrapped in a beach towel.

A Florida man contesting a speeding ticket brought the road’s speed-limit sign into traffic court Monday, saying its absence should have prevented him from receiving the ticket.

He first presented a photograph of an empty metal pole. When the magistrate asked when the picture had been taken, he said Saturday, three days after the traffic stop. He then unwrapped the sign and leaned it against the witness stand.

“Thirty miles an hour,” the magistrate read.

“Not anymore,” the man said.

He explained that he had removed the sign himself because he needed the photograph to show what he was talking about. The officer who issued the ticket confirmed that the sign had been standing during the stop.

The man asked whether the court could consider only the photograph. When an officer took possession of the sign, he requested a receipt, saying he had left a deposit on the truck he rented to transport it.

He then asked whether returning the sign could count as community service.
